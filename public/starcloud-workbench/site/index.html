One loop for orbital GPU ops.

The workbench turns a workload spec and platform profile into an inspectable receipt — power, thermal, downlink, and terrestrial baseline in one trail.

  1. 01 Plan

    Bind workload FLOPs, memory, and downlink bytes to SC-1 or SC-2 public specs.

  2. 02 Run

    Simulate solar/eclipse, battery SoC, GPU power, and junction temperature over time.

  3. 03 Doctor

    Flag thermal overrun, throttle, battery depletion, and ship-raw vs process-on-orbit.

  4. 04 Report

    Emit manifest JSON, summary, markdown report, and timeline CSV under out/receipts/.
