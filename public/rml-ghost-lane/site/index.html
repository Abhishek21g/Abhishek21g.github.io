Oracle rules

What Ghost Lane catches without sim

keep_out_violation

Waypoint inside declared crater-rim or boulder keep-out

forbidden_terrain

Sand ripple, cliff, boulder field. SPOC terrain class mismatch.

segment_too_long

Blind-drive segment > ~10 m between waypoints

slope_violation

Waypoint slope exceeds rover envelope

energy_budget_exceeded

Rough Wh estimate over sol drive budget

ai_hallucinated_command

Unknown RML tags from LLM schema drift

heading_discontinuity

AutoNav recovery risk from heading jumps
