1Plan

Define scenario, invariants, and expected behavior.

2Run

Execute upload/index/search/chat checks (mock or live).

3Doctor

Apply rules to detect indexing backlog and quality regressions.

4Report

Publish a shareable receipt bundle for CI and incident review.
