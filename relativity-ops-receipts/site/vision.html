Judging question

What did we build, and why?

We built a live runner, scenario library, and benchmark dashboard for testing whether a synthetic additive-manufacturing lifecycle stays coherent from design release through NDT, flight release, integration, and sea transport. Each profile gives the planner a DAG, deterministic schedule, and doctor rules that catch the coordination failures Factory Platform engineers describe publicly.

We built it because Relativity's public bet is not "we 3D-print parts" — it is proving a compilable digital thread at vehicle rate. The only honest way to demonstrate that discipline outside their factory is to ship open receipts with explicit assumptions.
